Mystery research works best when curiosity has a method. A strange signal, a recurring symbol, or a story passed through decades can be compelling, but the work becomes useful only when the archive is structured.

<Callout>
The goal is not to flatten wonder. The goal is to preserve enough context that wonder can survive contact with evidence.
</Callout>

## Build the first layer

Start with the simplest facts:

- who reported the signal
- when it appeared
- where it was observed
- what was actually recorded
- what changed between retellings

This turns speculation into a navigable map. From there, compare reports against environmental context, cultural incentives, and technological limits.
